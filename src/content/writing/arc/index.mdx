I've always wanted to build my personal website from scratch.<SideNote>I wrote this in July 2023 during college. The website is no longer overengineered and uses Astro + MDX now. The content remains unchanged.</SideNote> Something that would feel entirely my own, from its color scheme to the tech stack. So, I did!

In ways this website is over-engineered, took an insane amount of time, got super frustrating but it did teach me invaluable lessons, especially over project management.

## The Why.

Throughout life we all get to have countless unique experiences that ultimately die with us and hence this blog.

## The How.

I built this using NextJS, Sanity.io and TailwindCSS. If those words look alien to you:

[NextJS](https://nextjs.org/) — Provides a platform for data to be presented in a structured way.

[TailwindCSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) — Styles everything on the website.

[Sanity.io](https://www.sanity.io/) — Manages and feeds data to NextJS.

If you'd like to look deeper under the hood, here is a [link](https://github.com/AbhigyanBafna/abhigyan). While you are at it, do check out [Lee Robinson](https://leerob.io/), [Tushar Nankani](https://blog.tusharnankani.com/), and [William Thomson's](https://www.willthomson.dev/) websites. They served as a great source of inspiration for mine.

## The What.

I look at this as my personal space to share everything that intrigues me — observations, learnings, experiences, reflections, creations… anything that has something to offer to you. Also whether it's tech-related or not, I'll try my best to explain everything in layman terms.

The best part? Apart from being a platform to share things, this also acts as a time capsule for me to look back at my life.

So, welcome to my Arc. I hope you'll enjoy it :)
